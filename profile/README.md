# 🚀 Welcome to Alkami Hackathon 2026

We’re excited to have you here! 🎉

Whether you’re a client, a member of one of our partner teams, or an Alkami Advisor, you’re part of a community coming together to build, experiment, and imagine what’s next. Think big, move fast, and have fun along the way. ✨

## 🛠️ Before you begin

Please make sure your local development environment is ready before the hackathon starts. Bring the tools, accounts, repositories, credentials, and dependencies you expect to need so you can spend your time creating—not troubleshooting setup. 💻

If you run into hardware or equipment issues, please work with your assigned advisors. They’re here to help you unblock quickly and keep your momentum going. 🤝

## 💡 Hackathon mode: ON

- Have fun and enjoy the ride. 😄
- Share ideas early, often, and openly.
- Collaborate across teams, roles, and areas of expertise.
- Challenge the status quo and question assumptions. 🔥
- Start with a real problem and build toward a meaningful possibility.
- Prototype boldly—rough demos are welcome, and failure is data. 🧪
- Keep the environment inclusive, respectful, and welcoming for everyone.

## 🌟 Build something worth talking about

You don’t need to have everything figured out on day one. The goal is to explore, learn, and turn promising ideas into something you can share. Progress, curiosity, and teamwork matter just as much as the final demo.

Bring your curiosity. Bring your perspective. Bring the idea that sounds a little crazy—but might just work. 🚀

Good luck, have fun, and let’s build what’s next!

— The Alkami Hackathon Team
